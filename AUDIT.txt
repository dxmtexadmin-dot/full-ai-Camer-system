Quyidagilar bo‘yicha gumon qilish mumkin, lekin tekshiruvda XATO TOPILMADI:

  * Log rotatsiyasi BOR: RotatingFileHandler 5 MB × 5 nusxa

    monitor.stop()), monitoring_service.py:101-116 (thread join, timeout bilan).
  * Session cookie `httponly` — Starlette SessionMiddleware uni sukut bo‘yicha
    qo‘yadi (kodda faqat `same_site` va `https_only` ustidan yozilgan).
  * CSRF: `hmac.compare_digest` bilan (utils/security.py:csrf_valid), har POST da
    `deps.verify_csrf` (deps.py:111-114).
  * Open-redirect himoyasi BOR: `safe_next_url` (utils/security.py).
  * Parol xeshi: PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 260 000 iteratsiya.
  * Xatolar brauzerga stack trace chiqarmaydi (main.py:98-142, 5 handler).
  * /api/docs production da o‘chiriladi (main.py:65).
  * .env git da kuzatilmaydi (.gitignore:2-4), faqat .env.example bor.
  * SQLite sozlamalari to‘g‘ri: WAL, synchronous=NORMAL, busy_timeout=30 000,
    check_same_thread=False, timeout=30 (database.py).
  * Indekslar yetarli: asset_code (unique+index), AssetQrCode.token (unique),
    "ix_asset_assignment_open", Event.dedupe_key (unique),
    AssetMovementEvent.dedupe_key (unique).
  * Vaqt mintaqasi bilan ishlash to‘g‘ri: UTC saqlanadi, `as_utc()` SQLite ning
    tzinfo yo‘qotishini qoplaydi (utils/time_utils.py:38-42).

    MIN_MM=20 (10 mm o‘lcham o‘qilmasligi tuzatilgan), PRINT_DPI=300.
